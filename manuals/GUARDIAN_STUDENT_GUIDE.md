# PART A: THE GUARDIAN PORTAL (অভিভাবক পোর্টাল)
*A dedicated mobile-first experience for parents and family caregivers.*

## 1. Simple & Secure Guardian Login (অভিভাবক লগইন)
1. Open your school's web address (e.g., `https://kidsshine.educationos.com/login`).
2. Select the **Guardian (অভিভাবক)** tab.
3. Enter your registered mobile phone number and institutional password.
4. Tap **Log In**. You will enter the personalized Guardian Portal.

## 2. Multi-Child Sibling Switcher (একাধিক সন্তানের তথ্য পরিবর্তন)
- If you have two or more children enrolled in the same school (e.g., *Sami in Class 5* and *Zara in Class 2*), an interactive **Sibling Selector** appears at the top of your screen:
  `[ Sami Hossain (Class 5) ]   [ Zara Hossain (Class 2) ]`
- Tap a child's name to instantly switch the dashboard context. All attendance figures, exam reports, and fee records update to that specific child.

## 3. Real-Time Attendance Monitoring (দৈনিক উপস্থিতি ও পরিসংখ্যান)
- **Attendance Rate Gauge:** View your child's overall attendance rate (e.g., *94.2% Attendance*).
- **Working Days Breakdown:** View total school days, days present, and days absent.
- **Attendance Calendar:** Review monthly calendar with color-coded badges:
  - 🟢 **Present (উপস্থিত)**
  - 🔴 **Absent (অনুপস্থিত)**
  - 🟡 **Late (দেরি)**
- **Absentee Follow-Up:** If your child is marked absent, school coordinators will reach out via direct phone call or WhatsApp to confirm health and well-being.

## 4. Academic Transcripts & Progress Reports (পরীক্ষার ফলাফল)
- When examinations are published, view your child's terminal report card directly on your smartphone:
  - Subject-by-subject written, MCQ, and practical marks.
  - Official NCTB letter grades (A+, A, A-, B, C, D, F).
  - Term Grade Point Average (GPA) and Class Teacher observations.

## 5. Tuition Fees & Cash Receipts (বেতন ও ফি বিবরণী)
- View current term tuition fees, examination fees, and outstanding dues balance.
- Check payment history and view official duplicate cash receipts issued by the school cashier.
- *Notice:* Online digital payment (bKash/Nagad checkout) is under preparation for Stage 12.6 commercial launch; fee collections are currently recorded by the school finance counter with instant receipt updates on your portal.

# PART B: THE STUDENT PORTAL (শিক্ষার্থী পোর্টাল)
*An engaging, focused study dashboard tailored for learners.*

## 1. Student Login (শিক্ষার্থী লগইন)
1. Navigate to `/login` and select **Student (শিক্ষার্থী)**.
2. Enter your student account identifier and password.
3. You will be routed directly to your **Study Room & Learner Dashboard**.

## 2. Daily Routine & Timetable (দৈনিক ক্লাস রুটিন)
- Review today's period schedule:
  - Period start and end times.
  - Subject name and textbook chapter.
  - Assigned teacher and classroom/hall number.
- Always know your next class without carrying physical paper routines.

## 3. Personal Attendance Record (নিজের উপস্থিতি রেকর্ড)
- Check your personal attendance percentage and attendance streak.
- *Security Guard:* You can only view your own attendance data. Classmates' private attendance records are never visible.

## 4. Report Cards & Study Standing (ফলাফল ও মূল্যায়ন)
- Review official published grades and subject scores.
- Compare your performance across Half Yearly, Annual, and Pre-Test examinations.

## 5. School Notice Board (স্কুল নোটিশ বোর্ড)
- Read official institutional circulars regarding holidays, sports day, exam routines, and extra-curricular clubs.
- All administrative, teacher management, and financial fee collection controls are completely hidden to keep your dashboard clean and focused on learning.
