### The Executive Challenge
Running a forward-looking educational institution today is significantly hindered by administrative fragmentation. Teachers spend hundreds of hours manually marking registers, composing repetitive test questions, and compiling term results. Academic coordinators struggle to enforce national curriculum standards across parallel sections. Administrative teams reconcile physical cash counters against spreadsheets. Parents demand real-time visibility into their children's progress, while school boards face mounting data privacy liabilities.

Conventional School Management Systems (SMS) and ERPs attempted to digitize paperwork, but they created rigid, complicated software that frustrates faculty and fails to improve academic quality.

### The EducationOS Solution
**EducationOS is the modern operating system designed for the entire educational lifecycle.** 

Rather than merely storing files, EducationOS unites institutional leadership, administration, faculty, students, and guardians into a single, high-speed, intelligent digital ecosystem. Built on modern cloud technologies and integrated with localized pedagogical artificial intelligence, EducationOS automates routine operational tasks so educators can refocus their energy where it matters most: inspiring students and driving academic excellence.

---

### Key Capabilities at a Glance

1. **Intelligent Curriculum & Exam Blueprints (AI-Native)**
   - **Board-Standard Question Composer:** Automatically formats multiple-choice questions (MCQ) and creative question (CQ) stems adhering to national curriculum (NCTB) rules in pure Bengali and formal English.
   - **Educator Review Guarantee:** AI-generated materials are held as `Drafts` until authorized by teachers, preserving complete human pedagogical authority.
   - **Socratic Student Guidance:** An embedded AI tutor provides hints and guiding questions without ever solving homework or exam questions directly.

2. **1-Tap Attendance & Offline Continuity (PWA Technology)**
   - **Fast Classroom Roll Calls:** Faculty record section attendance in under 30 seconds using any smartphone, tablet, or laptop.
   - **Zero Internet Interruptions:** If classroom Wi-Fi or mobile networks drop, attendance data persists locally in secure browser storage and flushes automatically once connectivity resumes.
   - **Installable Experience:** Installs cleanly directly from the browser onto devices without requiring cumbersome third-party app store downloads.

3. **Double-Entry Tuition Ledgers & Admissions**
   - **Dual-Model Admissions:** Complete support for both open direct admissions and competitive entrance exam screening.
   - **Instant Dual-Copy Receipts:** Automated receipt generation with support for cash counters, bank deposits, and mobile money (bKash/Nagad) tracking.
   - **Audit-Proof Ledgers:** Complete transparency and dual-custody approval workflows to prevent institutional revenue leakage.

4. **NCTB Grading Engine & One-Click Report Cards**
   - **Standard 7-Tier Grading:** Configured for official letter grades ($A+$, $A$, $A-$, $B$, $C$, $D$, $F$) and GPA computations.
   - **Print-Ready Terminal Cards:** Instantly generate clean, publication-grade A4 terminal progress reports with custom school crests and signatures.

5. **Institutional Security & Complete Data Privacy**
   - **PostgreSQL Native Row-Level Security (RLS):** Every query enforces tenant boundaries at the database engine level, mathematically preventing one school's records from leaking into another.
   - **Strict Zero-Monetization Policy:** Your institutional records belong exclusively to your school. EducationOS never sells, licenses, or displays advertisements against student or guardian data.
